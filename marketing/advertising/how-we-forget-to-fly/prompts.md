The initial v03 kite entered the reserved upper-right region. A separate ImageGen edit used only that first v03 PNG as the edit target. The corrected PNG is the delivered v03; the uncorrected result is not a campaign candidate.

Edit this exact square banner. Preserve every word, typography, layout, colors, paper figures, book, CTA, wind ribbons and image dimensions.
